#Chemistry/Atmosphere 
# Process
- Energy from the sun travels to the earth as short wavelength [[Electromagnetic Radiation]]. Some is reflected by the atmosphere but most transmit This is because short wavelength doesn't interact strongly with gas molecules in the atmosphere.
- The energy of radiation is absorbed when it reaches the surface of the earth.
- The earth now *re-emits*/radiates the energy as long wavelength radiation - *infrared*
- Some of this is reflected by the gas molecules in the atmosphere.

**Greenhouse gases**
- $H_2O$
- $CH_4$
- $CO_2$
- $NO_2$
- $O_3$
- $SO_2$
