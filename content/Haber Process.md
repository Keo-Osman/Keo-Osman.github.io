#Chemistry/Resources 
# Reaction






$$
3H_2+N_2\Leftrightarrow 2NH_3
$$






Passed over iron catalyst at $450\degree C$ and $200 atm$ pressure. Forward reaction is [[Exothermic and Endothermic Reactions#Exothermic|Exothermic]] 

# Yield Increase
To increase yield the ammonia is cooled into a liquid and removed. The unreacted nitrogen and hydrogen are recycled back over the catalyst.
- Temperature - $450\degree C$ is a compromise as higher temperatures shift the equilibrium to the left as the forward reaction is exothermic but we need a high temperature for a fast rate of reaction. $450\degree C$ is an optimal compromise.
- Iron catalyst - increase rate of reaction. *No effect on equilibrium position*
- Pressure - high pressure pushes equilibrium to right as there is less molecules so pressure is less so system moves there to counteract. High pressures can be expensive and dangerous so $200 atm$ is a good compromise.
