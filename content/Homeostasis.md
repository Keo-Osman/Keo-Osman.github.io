#Biology/Homeostasis-and-Response 
# Definition
**Homeostasis** is the regulation of the *internal conditions* of a [[Cells|Cell]] or organism to maintain *optimum conditions* for function in *response* to *internal or external changes*

# Automatic Control System
Homeostasis mainly happens through **Automatic Control Systems**.
These consist of **Receptor Cells, Co-Ordination Centre** and an **Effector**.
1. First the receptor cells detect a stimulus
2. The receptor cells pass information to the co-ordination centre
3. The co-ordination centre processes this information
4. The co-ordination centre then sends out a signal to an effector
5. The effector carries out a response to return/get closer to optimum conditions
