#English/Jekyll-Hyde
# Chapter 1 - Story of The Door
We meet [[Utterson]] and Enfield recounts the story when he saw 2 figures, a man and young girl. Then "The man trampled calmly over the girl" Enfield and a doctor threaten to ruin his reputation unless they get £100. The man goes into the building and returns with gold and a cheque. Enfield doesn't trust the man and tells Utterson that the man was called [[Hyde]]      
# Chapter 2 -  Search for Mr Hyde
Utterson goes to his safe and takes [[Jekyll]]'s will which makes [[Hyde]] the beneficiary of his wealth. Utterson goes to [[Lanyon]]'s place. Jekyll and Lanyon have had a disagreement about their [[Jekyll and Hyde Themes#Science|Science]]. Utterson hangs around the side street near the door in order to see [[Hyde]]. Utterson loathes him and begins to fear for Jekyll. Poole tells Utterson about Hyde and how they have orders to obey him and that Hyde comes and goes as he pleases.
# Chapter 3 - Dr Jekyll Was Quite at Ease
*Story skips forward 2 weeks.* [[Utterson]] reminds [[Jekyll]] of the will and how much he disapproves of it, Jekyll dismisses his concern
# Chapter 4 - The Carew Murder case
The chapter opens with the description of the murder of Carew we learn from a maid's POV. [[Hyde]] clubs Carew to his death. Utterson takes the police to Hyde's address. They notice that the place is luxuriously furnished but also the place has recently been ransacked.
# Chapter 5 - Incident of the Letter
[[Utterson]] asks [[Jekyll]] about Carew's murder. Utterson asks if Jekyll is hiding the murderer, Jekyll denies and says that no more will ever be heard form [[Hyde]]. Jekyll admits that he's receive a letter from Hyde that says he has escaped and will never return. Utterson analyses the letter with Guest and they declare that both were written by the same person with an attempt of disguise.
# Chapter 6 - Remarkable incident of Dr Lanyon
[[Utterson]] visits [[Lanyon]] who is very ill and says he has had a shock and will die soon, Lanyon dies 2 weeks after this. The night after his funereal Utterson opens a letter from Lanyon which contains another letter which says to no to be opened until the death or disappearance of [[Jekyll]] 
# Chapter 7 - Incident at the Window
[[Utterson]] and Enfield walked passed the window which they saw at the start, they see [[Jekyll]] who says that he will die soon. Mid conversation Jekyll transforms into [[Hyde]] and a look of terror comes over him and he quickly pulls down the blind.
# Chapter 8 - The Last Night
In the evening [[Utterson]] is interrupted by Poole who says he is worried that [[Jekyll]] has been murdered. They arrive at Jekyll's chambers and Poole tells him that Utterson is there to see him. A voice responds that is not Jekyll's saying that he can't see them. Both Poole and Utterson return to the kitchen and Poole says he believes that Jekyll has been murdered as he heard lots of screaming and shouting. Poole says that the voice was asking for a specific medicine that Jekyll was previously frantic about getting. Poole takes an axe and Utterson takes a poker and they break through the door and the see [[Hyde]] wearing clothes that are too big and Utterson realises that Hyde has taken a substance to kill himself
# Chapter 9 - Dr Lanyon's Narrative
[[Lanyon]] explains in a letter to [[Utterson]] that he received a letter from [[Jekyll]] asking for him to come to his chambers and go in alone and retrieve a specific drawer. At midnight a man meets Lanyon who already hates him. The man is desperate for the contents in the drawer. The man then makes a potion from the ingredients and drinks it and turns into Jekyll  
# Chapter 10 - Henry Jekyll's Full Statement of the Case
[[Jekyll]] explain the events of the story to [[Utterson]] in a letter. Jekyll begins by describing his background. He is heir to a large fortune with a desire to work and be seen as good so he concealed his pleasures and discuses the dual nature of humans and how people restrain themselves for "decency" he made potions to sperate these 2 sides. He enjoyed being [[Hyde]] and he tells all of his servants to let Hyde to do what he wants and he draws up the will leaving everything to Hyde, the anger of Hyde scares Jekyll and he stops being Hyde for a while but he transforms into Hyde against his will one day, he arranges with [[Lanyon]] to get the powder from his chambers. After this he realises that Hyde has take over and Jekyll has to take the potion to stop himself from becoming Hyde. Jekyll and Hyde are battling for power and hate each other. But the potions stop working and he can't change back from Hyde. Jekyll in the end says that this is the *"hour of his death"* and that he doesn't care what happens to Hyde afterwards
