#Biology/Inheritance-and-Evolution 
# Process
- Desired genes are isolate/cut out from chromosomes using [[Enzymes]].
- The gene is transferred to a bacterial plasmid or virus *(vector)*.
- The vector inserts the gene into the required cells during the development stage of the organism which the genes are being transferred to.
- The organism will develop with the new characteristic.
