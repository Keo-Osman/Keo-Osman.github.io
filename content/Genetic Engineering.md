# Process
1. Desired genes are isolate/cut out from chromosomes using [[Enzymes]].
2. The gene is transferred to a bacterial plasmid or virus *(vector)*.
3. The vector inserts the gene into the required cells during the development stage of the organism which the genes are being transferred to.
4. The organism will develop with the new characteristic.
