#Chemistry/Atmosphere 
# Coal
- $CO_2$ from photosynthesis ends up in fossil fuels
- Coals is formed from the remains of *ferns and trees*. If they die in marshy wetlands they do not decompose this can be due to lack of oxygen of acidic conditions which prevents bacteria form carrying out decomposition.
- Over time they are compressed by sediment forming coal.

# Oil
- Formed by plankton
- When they die they settle in mud on the sea-bed
- If no oxygen is present they don't decompose
- Overtime they are compressed by sediment forming crude oil.

# Natural Gas
- Mainly methane.
- Formed by plankton similar to oil.
- The carbon in $CH_4$ was part of the $CO_2$ taken in by photosynthesis.
