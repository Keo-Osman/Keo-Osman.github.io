# Graphene
It is a single layer of graphite.
Properties:
1. Good conductor of electricity - delocalised electros
2. Strong with high melting point - lots of strong covalent bonds
# Fullerene
1. Have hollow shapes
2. Have rings of 5, 6 or 7 carbon atoms
## Buckminsterfullerene $C_{60}$
1. Arranged in sphere
2. Carbon forms rings of 5 or 6 carbon atoms
3. Uses
	1. Can deliver drugs
	2. Lubricants in machines
	3. Catalysts
## Carbon Nanotubes
1. High length : diameter ration
2. Rings of 6 carbon atoms
3. Properties
	1. High tensile strength
	2. Delocalised electrons for conduction of electricity
	3. Good conductor of heat
4. Uses
	5. Reinforce materials
