# Conscientious Objectors
## WW1
In *March 1916* conscription was introduced and refusing to fight became illegal. However there was ~16k men called conscientious objectors who refused to enlist in the army. 
Reasons for objecting:
 1. Religious beliefs.
 2. Pacifists.
 3. Political beliefs.
If you objected because of your conscience you had to apply to a tribunal. These were often unfair as the jury was often ex-military.
**Absolutists** - Those who objected to any military orders or anything war related.
**Alternativists** - Those who refused to carry a weapon but were willing to help in other ways.

Absolutists were either imprisoned or given hard labour as a deterrent. The media portrayed them as lazy, greedy, feminine and homosexual.

## WW2
Conscription was immediately introduced and public attitude was still negative towards COs and tribunals were still required however.
1. Tribunal judges couldn't be ex-military.
2. Prison became a last resort punishment as the government didn't want to seem like the Nazis.
3. More alternate jobs that aligned with religious beliefs such as farm work were offered.

# Changing Definition of Crime
Crime rate was appearing to increase however this is because of the technology is much better for catching criminals.

1. Smuggling
	1. 20th Century: Items smuggled were now: tobacco, drugs, alcohol, people. Many things were smuggled because they were illegal sometimes to avoid tax. Most items items were smuggle via lorries, vans, drug mules etc..
	2. 18th Century: Items smuggled were now: brandy, tea, cloth. Many things were smuggled to avoid tax without it normal people could actually afford them allowing for big profits. Most items items were smuggle via sailing ships.
2. Sexuality
	1. *1967 Sexual Offences Act* - Homosexuality legalised. People became more tolerant.
3. Race
	1. *1968 Race Relations Act* - Made illegal to refuse jobs, housing or public based on race/ethnic background.
	2. *2006 Race Relations Act Extended* - Made illegal to spread hatred due to race/religion.
	3. To reduce hate towards minorities as the was an increased amount of terrorist acts linked to extreme religion.
4. Hate Crime
	1. *2005 Criminal Justice Act* - Hate crime would be given the same sentence as any other crime.
5. Women
	1. *1967 Abortion Act* - Legalised abortions for pregnancy's less than 28 weeks. To reduce major health problems from illegal abortions.
	2. *1976 Domestic Violence Act*
6. Drugs
	1. *1971 Drugs Misuse Act* - some classifications of drug became illegal.
