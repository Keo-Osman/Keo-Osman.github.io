#History/Elizabeth 
# Religious
*Link: [[Catholic Opposition]]*
## Problem
1. The majority of people were traditional and did not like religious change.
2. To many people the appearance of the church was important.
3. The clergy, bishops and church were Catholic whilst Elizabeth and her advisors were Protestant.
4. Protestants who fled under Mary I expected Elizabeth to return to Protestantism.
### Catholics vs Protestants
## What Elizabeth Did
1. She worshipped privately.
2. Allowed Catholics to live in England freely.
3. Introduced a fine of *1 shilling* per missed church service. However this wasn't strictly enforced.
### Religious Settlement
#### Act of Supremacy 1559
Elizabeth declared herself as head of the church rather then the pope officially calling herself *"Supreme governor of the church"*.
All MPs and clergy had to take an oath. If they refused they would put in prison. Refusing 3 times led to execution.
#### Act of Uniformity 1559
Elizabeth established what the churches should look like and what church services entail.
1. Kept the church looking Catholic as Catholics cared much more about the appearance whereas Protestants didn't mind.
2. Ornaments and decorations allowed.
3. Services to be in English.
4. Clergy had to wear vestments.
She sets up inspections and 1st time 400 clergies are dismissed.
### Responses
1. 8000/10000 clergy/priests agreed to it.
2. Only 1 bishop agreed the rest lost their title and Elizabeth appointed 27 new ones.
3. Most people transition quickly but it is slow in the north.
4. Protestants in London smash statues.
5. Pope *didn't* excommunicate her but he encouraged Catholics to not attend Protestant services.
6. Some Catholic priests held Catholic mass in private.

1. There was bad quality of harvests.

## What Elizabeth Did
Elizabeth didn't do much. In 1558 the harvests were very good which allowed food prices to fall. *She got lucky*.

# [[16th Century England#Privy Council|Privy Council]]
## Problem
1. She wants to choose *Protestant* noble men however she needs some *Catholic* noble men as a large portion of the country was still Catholic.
2. Many nobles wanted to be on the Privy Council and would be upset if they weren't. *Elizabeth wants too keep the loyalty of the nobility.*
## What Elizabeth Did
1. Reduce the number of members from *50* to *19*.
2. Didn't make her closest friend *(Robert Dudley)* a member for 4 years. She was worried about about other nobles' jealously.
3. She made sure to keep *experienced* nobles on it.

# International Safety
## Problem
1. There was a war with France.
2. There was high religious tension between France and England.
3. France and Scotland had the ***Auld Alliance*** and an joint attack would be devastating.
4. High religious tension with Catholic Spain - the most powerful country in Europe.
## What Elizabeth Did
1. Spent *£100,000* on weapons and military such as guns bows and gunpowder. 
2. Signed a treaty with France in *Jan 1559*- 3 months after taking to power

---
# Financial Security
## Problem
1. She inherited a *£300,000* debt from *Henry VII's* war with France. Prices were rising as well so it was very difficult to pay the debt.
## What Elizabeth Did
1. Cut back government spending.
2. Sold land belonging to the crown for *£600,000* paying off all debts and building a reserve of *£300,000*.

# Question of Legitimacy
## Problem
1. Catholics didn't believe she was legitimate due to her mother *Anne Boleyn* being a 2nd wife of Henry VII and Catholics didn't see divorce as legitimate.
2. People believed that a female leader wouldn't be as capable.
## What Elizabeth Did
1. Had a big coronation to send a message that she was definitely queen.

# Marriage and Succession 
## Problem
1. People expected her to marry quickly but it was difficult to find someone and there was question over whether she should marry an English or foreign man.
2. She had no successors/heirs.
3. Privy Council were divided on the issue.
## What Elizabeth Did
1. She declined King Phillip II of Spain and Eric of Sweden.
2. She often said as a metaphor that she was "Married to England".
