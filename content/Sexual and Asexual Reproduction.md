# Sexual Reproduction
*Sexual Reproduction* involves the joining of male and female **gametes**, each containing genetic information from the mother or father.
*Gametes* are formed by [[Meiosis]]. Gametes are formed by meiosis, as they are non identical. Each gamete has 23 chromosomes and they fuse in fertilisation. The genetic information from each parent is mixed, producing variation in the offspring.

---
# Asexual Reproduction
*Asexual Reproduction* involves one parent with **NO** gametes joining. It happens using the process of [[Cell Division#Mitosis|Mitosis]] therefore there is no mixing of genetic information. It leads to *clones*, which are *genetically identical to each other and the parent*.

---
# Advantages and Disadvantages
## Sexual Reproduction
- Variation in offspring - *allows higher probability of characteristic survival advantage, decreases chance of species-wide extinction*
- Allows use of *Selective Breeding*.
## Asexual Reproduction
- Only one parent needed.
- Less energy and is faster - do not need to find a mate.
- Lots of offspring can be produced.
