# How Hitler Took Control of The DAP
1. Hitler was a corporal in WW1 after he recovered in hospital he was employed to monitor political activates. 
2. The *DAP* (German worker's party) was lead by *Anton Drexler*. 
3. Hitler had to attended their party meetings for his job and he was attracted to their ideas.
4. Hitler took over the *DAP* and turned it into the *NSDAP*
## 25 Point Programme
The 25 point programme was a document explaining the policies of the NSDAP. Most polices were nationalist or socialist and were for a stronger Germany there was only a few mentions of antisemitism. This resonated with people who were in a weak Germany after signing the ToV.
Some policies included:
1. Union of all Germans in Greater Germany.
2. Land and colonies to feed people and settle surplus of population.
3. The states primary duty must be to promote work and the livelihood of its citizens.
4. Only those of German blood are members of the nation. No jew may be a member of the nation.
## Hitlers Personal Appeal
1. He gained support by delivering well rehearsed public speeches with lots of hand gestures and emotion.
2. He increase DAP members from 1,000 to 3,000 in ~6 months.

# Why The Nazi Party Failed to Gain Popularity 1924-29
The NSDAP had practically no support form the working class and only gained 1% of the votes in Berlin and the Ruhr industrial district.
## What Hitler Did to Strengthen The Nazi Party
1. Gain financial backing. Raised from wealthy individuals who shared Hitler's nationalist and industrial views. However this made him more accepting of capitalistic ideas.
2. Restructure the party to include all Germans. Created: *German Women's Order, Nationalist Socialist German Student's League, School Pupil's League*.
3. Make the NSDAP a national party.
4. Ensure loyalty of paramilitary forces. Expand SA to *400,000* members, replaces Rohm, sets up SS.
5. Unite the party and ensure total agreement with his ideas. Hitler holds the *Bamberg Conference* and speaks for hours. *Goebbels* is promoted for abandoning socialism and Strasser is murdered in 1934 for not agreeing with the Nazi party ideas.
## Reasons for Limited Support
1. [[Weimar Germany#Gustav Stresemann|Stresemann's]] actions led to economic stability and high employment which restored faith in democracy so people were less inclined to vote for an extremist party.
2. in *1925* Paul von Hindenburg became president and people didn't want to vote against a war hero.

# Great Depression 1929
## Cause
1. Wall street crash leading to German banks collapsing as they had large investments in American stocks and they had taken large loans form America who is now asking for it back.
2. Loans had to be paid back so wages were cut which led to falling sales which led to rising unemployment and there was less demand causing a cycle of depression.
3. People have little amounts of money so they can't pay many taxes so benefits are cut leading to even more unemployment and homelessness furthering the cycle.

# Why The Nazi Party Gained Support
1. Middle Class:
	1. Angry that the government hadn't done anything to protect savings during hyperinflation and great depression.
	2. Lost faith in moderate parties as weak coalitions couldn't agree on much so Article 48 was abused to pass all policies.
	3. Many Christians disgusted at the change in culture.
	4. Hitler promised to protect them from communism and he has the army of 400,000 SA to do it.
2. Working Class:
	1. Workers impressed by the organisation of th NSDAP.
	2. The SA and rallies were impressive.
	3. The propaganda posters promised "work and bread".
3. Women:
	1. The propaganda posters pushed the view of the radiational family and the importance of traditional roles of women.
	2. Hitler's speeches made women fell that they were one of the most important people in Germany.
4. Young People:
	1. Parades and rallies were colourful and exciting.
	2. *Nationalist Socialist German Student's League*.
5. Business:
	1. Weimar government was a mess during the great depression and failed to sort out any problem.
	2. Hitler offered protection form communism and they had lots of anti-communist propaganda.
6. Farmers:
	1. Famers gained little during the golden age and struggled with great poverty.
	2. The Nazi party changed policy about land to interest farmers.
	3. Hitler promised to get rid of the ToV and many farmers would gain land back from that.
