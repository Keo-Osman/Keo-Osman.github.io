#History/Cold-War
# Marshall Plan
- USA wanted to contain the spread of communism *(Truman doctrine)* believing that if they could stop one country from becoming communist it would have a domino effect and stop other countries from following suit and becoming communist.
- Marshall - US secretary of state - believed that communism fed on poverty and destroyed economies.
- The USA provided, air to war-torn Europe in the form of money, machinery, food and technological assistance in return for the countries buying US goods.
- The Marshall Plan aimed to: raise living standards, rebuild Germany, weaken soviet control of eastern Europe, help US economy.
- The Marshall aid was also offered to the USSR and the satellite sates but Stalin refused for the USSR and on behalf of the satellite states

In response Stalin called it "dollar imperialism" and made the following:
**Cominform**
- Aimed to co-ordinate communist parties in Europe
- Set up a collective plan - satellite sates had to follow soviet aims.

**Comecon**
- Plan of financial aid form USSR
- Encouraged countries to specialise in a particular economic area - made them heavily reliant on import and trades
- Set up eastern European trade network. They can't trade with the USA and West.
# Arms Race
- 1945 USA develops atomic bomb
- 1949 USSR develops the atomic bomb
- 1952 USA tests hydrogen bomb
- 1953 USSR tests hydrogen bomb
- 1957 USA and USSR test ICMBs - could travel up to 4500km

**MAD** - Mutually assured destruction
There was extreme amounts of spending which in 1953 reached $49.6b for USA and $25.5b (just for 1953)
Both sides wanted to reduce spending but an agreement could not be made.
# Berlin Crisis 1958-1963
West Germany was thriving whilst East Germany was suffering as they were very poor under communism and lots of their resources were being siphoned out to the USSR via **Comecon**- collective farms. This led to a shortage of goods many of which could be cheaply bought in West Germany.
Many people left East Germany to go to the West for a better quality of life and opportunities. By 1958 3 million had left, by 1961 20,000 leaving a month many of whom were skilled workers this was called the **Brain Drain**

**Khrushchev's Response - Berlin Ultimatum**
He demanded that:
- Berlin should be demilitarised
- Berlin should be a free city and run as it's own independent state.
The US saw it as an attempt to spread communism and if western troops left Berlin the Red Army would immediately take over.
The led to a series of talks.

- **Geneva, May 1959**
	- Foreign ministers talked about future of Germany but no agreement was made.
	- No agreement.
- **Camp David, Sept 1959**
	- Eisenhower and Khrushchev talked amount future of Germany but no agreement was made.
	- Khrushchev withdraws ultimatum.
	- No agreement.
- **Paris, May 1960**
	- Talked about the US U-2 spy plane that was shot down in USSR. Embarrassment for US, USSR angry.
	- Khrushchev walks out.
	- No agreement.
- **Vienna, Jun 1961**
	- Kennedy president, Khrushchev saw him as inexperienced and weak but Kennedy did not give into his demands.
	- Kennedy increased US spending by $2b.
	- No agreement.

**Berlin Wall**
On August 12th 1961 a barbed fence was placed by German troops on the border of East and West Berlin. There was a 150m buffer zone as to not anger the US.


# Carter Doctrine
US President Carter was under a lot of pressure from the seizure of US embassy staff in Iran and the soviet invasion of Afghanistan so he took a tough approach to the USSR called the carter doctrine.
He stated that:
- The USA would use military force to defend it's national interest in the Persian Gulf.
- Provide military aid to countries bordering Afghanistan.
- Delay Salt II and end Détente.
- US boycott of Moscow Olympics. 
# Gorbachev's New Thinking
- Gorbachev inherited a lot of problems from the USSR such as:
	- Poor living standard.
	- Weak economy from spending in arms race.
	- Reliant of military and secret police.

His new methods included:
- Reform the soviet economy allowing some capitalistic aspects to allow more trade - **Perestroika**.
- Be more open to public and relax censorship - **Glasnost**.
- Allow the soviet satellite states to have more freedom, reversing the Brezhnev doctrine.
- Reducing spending on weapons and withdraw from Afghanistan.

The USSR and USA had a series of talks:

- **Geneva, Nov 1985**
	- Gorbachev and Reagan met for the first time.
	- No agreement.
- **Reykjavik, Oct 1986**
	- Talked about reducing nuclear weapons and SDI.
	- No agreement.
- **Washington, Dec 1987**
	- Talked about disarmament.
	- Signed INF treaty abolish land based missiles with range 500-550km
- **Moscow, 1988**
	- Resolved INF
	- USSR announced reduction in Warsaw act troops in Afghanistan.
- **Malta, 1989**
	- Gorbachev met new president George Bush.
