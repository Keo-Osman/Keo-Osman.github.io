# Prime Counting Function
$\pi(n) = \text{the number of primes } \leq n$
For any $m \in \mathbb{Z}$, $\nexists p(x)$ with integer coefficients s.t $p(n)$ is prime $\forall n \geq m$


$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\pi(n)}{n/\ln(n)} = 1
$$

# Division Properties
1. Let $p$ be prime, $1 \leq k < p \Rightarrow p|\binom{p}{k}$
2. For a prime $p$, $p$ uniquely divides $n$ if $p^k || n$ means $p^k|n$ is the greatest $k$ s.t $p^k|n$
