# Definition
Fractional Distillation is the process of breaking [[Crude Oil]] into separate **Fractions** which is a useful mixture containing similar length chain [[Hydrocarbons#Alkenes|Alkenes]] and [[Hydrocarbons#Alkanes|Alkenes]].
During the fractional distillation of **Crude Oil**:
1. Heated crude oil enters a tall fractionating column, which is hot at the bottom and gets cooler towards the top
2. Vapours from the oil rise through the column
3. Vapours condense when they become cool enough
4. Liquids are led out of the column at different heights

[[Hydrocarbons#Short vs Long Chain Hydrocarbons|Short Chain]] are found nearer the top due to lower boiling point, whereas [[Hydrocarbons#Short vs Long Chain Hydrocarbons|Long Chain]] are found nearer the bottom.
