# How Hitler Became a Dictator 
## 1933
1. *Feb 27* - The Reichstag building was burned down by a *Dutch* communist. Hitler used this as an opportunity to blame it on a larger communist conspiracy and arrest *4000* communists.
2. *Feb 28* - Hitler persuaded Hindenburg to pass an emergency decree using *Article 48* called *The Decree for the Protection of the People and the State*. This allowed the Nazi party to imprison large numbers of their political opponents.
3. *March* - Hitler called an election and the NSDAP gained 93 seats and were still the largest party with 43.9% of the vote. They joined with the DNVP the form a coalition giving them a 51.9% majority. *(However this was not the 2/3 majority needed to change the constitution)*.
4. *March* - Hitler proposed the *Enabling Act* to the Reichstag giving him the right to pass laws without the Reichstag for 4 years. He then surrounded the voting chamber with members of the SA intimidating people to voting yes and they wouldn't let any members of the communist party in to vote. It passed with 88% of votes.
5. *May* - Hitler order the Nazis to break into trade union offices and arrest trade union officials. He then used th enabling act to ban trade unions and strikes.
6. *July* - Hitler banned all other political parties.
7. *Nov* - Hitler holds an "election", being the only party they gain 39m votes.
## Night of the Long Knives (1934)
## Causes
1. The SA's leader was *Ernst Rohm* who was more socialist. He had merged the SA with an army veterans group now bringing the numbers to 3m but 60% were unemployed so were in favour of Rohm's ideas.
2. The leaders of the German army were concerned as they were limited to 100k by the ToV so the SA outnumbered them 30x so they along with the SS wanted to reduce the SA's power.
3. The leaders of the SS and the army told Hitler that Rohm was planning a takeover.
## Events
1. *30th June 1934* - Hitler arranged a meeting with Rohm and 100 other SA leaders at a hotel when they arrived Rohm and other SA leaders were arrested, imprisoned and shot.
2. Over a 4 day period 400 people were rounded up and who without trail including *Von Schlicher* and *Gustav von Car*.
3. *2nd July* - Goering gave a press conference saying that security had been monitoring Rohm for months and that the killings were "In the best interest of Germany" and that "Germany had been saved from a dangerous socialist".

3. Had 240k men.

## Gestapo
1. Lead by Reinhard Heydrich - under control of SS.
2. ***Secret*** police force.
3. Could arrest and sent anyone to a concentration camp under suspicion.
4. Could tap phone lines.
5. Given permission to torture.
6. 30k members *(people thought there were more)*
## Concentration Camps
1. Purpose was to imprison political opposition.
2. Anyone with traits deemed undesirable by the Nazis (such as: Jewish, homosexual) was sent there,
3. By 1939 they had 150,000 members.
4. Pointless hard labour or save labour.
5. High death rate and brutally treated but not intentionally killed.
