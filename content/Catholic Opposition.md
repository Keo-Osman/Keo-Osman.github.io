# Northern Earls Rebellion - 1569
## Reasons
1. They wanted more power.
	1. Nobles were not given role in the [[16th Century England#Privy Council|Privy Council]].
	2. Earl of Northumberland lost control of copper mine on his land.
2. They wanted England to be more Catholic.
	1. James Pilkington (a devout Protestant) was appointed as Archbishop of Durham becoming the most important clergymen in north England.
	2. Earl of Northumberland and Earl of Westmoreland had lost power, due to them being Catholic, in [[16th Century England#Queen's Court|Elizabeth's court]].
3. They saw MQS as the rightful monarch and Elizabeth as illegitimate.
## Plan
In 1568 Thomas Howard (Elizabeth's Cousin and Duke of Norfolk) planned to marry MQS however this was treason as they would normally needed Elizabeth's permission. In doing so they hoped this would force Elizabeth to marry or name a successor.
## What Happened
1. Duke of Norfolk admits to the plan and is put in the tower of London.
2. The Northern Earls are called to London but they disobey the Queens orders and refuse.
3. The Earl of Sussex (Elizabeth's Supporter) begins raising troops but he is having difficulty.
4. The Northern Earls seize Durham cathedral, destroy evidence of Protestantism and hold Catholic mass. They also manage to take control of large portions of North England.
5. Elizabeth helps the Earl of Sussex and they manage to raise an army of *14,000* and defeat the Northern Earls.
## Consequences
1. Earls of Northumberland and Earl of Westmoreland flee England.
2. Earls of Northumberland is caught and executed with his head being put on a spike as a display.
3. 450 other rebels are executed as warning.

# Ridolfi Plot - 1571
## Who Supported
1. Robert Ridolfi.
2. Duke of Norfolk.
3. King Phillip II of Spain.
4. The Pope.
5. Duke of Alba *(Spanish Noble)*.
## Plan
1. Duke of Alba supplies *10,000* troops.
2. Spanish Invasion.
3. Elizabeth Killed.
4. MQS put on throne.
5. Duke of Norfolk marries MQS.
## Consequences
1. Duke of Norfolk executed.
2. Ridolfi runs away.
3. No evidence of MQS' involvement.
4. ***2 laws against Catholics passed:***
	1. If anyone misses church they are fined £20 *(recusants)*.
	2. Made illegal to convert people to Catholicism.

# Throckmorton Plot - 1583
## Who Supported
1. Francis Throckmorton.
2. King Phillip II of Spain.
3. The Pope.
4. Duke of Guise *(French Noble)*.
5. ***List of practicing Catholics***
## Plan
1. Invade with an army. Troops supplied by France and financed by Spain.
2. Put MQS on the throne.
## Consequences
1. Throckmorton Executed.
2. No evidence of MQS' involvement.
3. 2 laws passed;
	1. Act for the Preservation of the Queen's Safety: If Elizabeth is assassinated, MQS won't be her heir.
	2. Anyone caught hiding a Catholic Priest is executed.

# Babington Plot - 1586
## Who Supported
1. Anthony Babington.
2. MQS.
3. King Phillip II of Spain.
4. Duke of Guise.
## Plan
1. Invade with 60,000 men.
2. Put MQS on Throne.
## Consequences
1. Babington and conspirators are hung, drawn and quartered.
2. MQS 
	1. *Oct 1586* put on trial by [[16th Century England#Privy Council|Privy Council]] and found guilty of high treason.
	2. *Feb 1587* Elizabeth signs MQS' death warrant.
