#Physics/Electromagnetism
# Alternator
An alternator is a coil that is rotating through a magnetic field and connected to 2 commutators which allow current to pass through the coil. It produces **AC** *(via the [[Generator Effect]])* as when the coil rotates the direction of each side changes.

# Dynamo 
A Dynamo is a coil that is rotating through a magnetic field and connected to a **split-ring commutator** which allow current to pass through the coil It has 2 different sides with  a gap as the coil rotates once it gas past 90 degrees the sides of the coil change which side of the split-ring commutator they are connected to so the side of the coil that is moving down always stays connected to the same side therefore it produces **DC** *(via the [[Generator Effect]])* as when the coil rotates the direction of each side changes.
