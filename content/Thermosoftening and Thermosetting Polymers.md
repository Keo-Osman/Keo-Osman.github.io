#Chemistry/Resources 
The properties of [[Polymers]] depend on both the *monomer* and the conditions used to make the polymer.

To change the properties of a polymer you could change:
- The temperature
- The pressure
- The catalyst

# Thermosoftening Polymers
The melt when heated and can be reshaped as the cool down.
The polymer chains are held by *inter-molecular forces*
# Thermosetting Polymers
They do not melt when heated.
They polymer chains are connected by *strong crosslinks* which DO NOT melt when heated.
