# Definition
Chromatography is a separation technique based on different solubilities. Different substances will be more attracted to the **mobile phase** - *the solvent* or the **stationary phase** *- the paper*.
The $R_f$ value of substance is a measure of solubility and is calculated with 

$$
\frac{\text{distance travelled by substance}}{\text{distance travelled by solvent}}
$$


since different substances will have different $R_f$ chromatography can be used to identify substances by looking up in a database of known measured $R_f$ values.

---
# Pure vs Impure
*Pure substances* will produce a single spot.
*Formulations* will produce multiple spots each corresponding to the a pure substance present in the formulation
