#Chemistry/Chemical-Analysis
# $H_2$
Insert a burning splint into the gas. If there is a squeaky pop a gas is present
# $O_2$ 
Insert a glowing splint in the gas. If it relights oxygen is present.
# $CO_2$ 
Put gas drawn from pipette into limewater - *aqueous solution of calcium hydroxide*. If it goes cloudy then carbon dioxide is present.
# $Cl_2$ 
Insert damp litmus paper into gas. If it is bleached white then chlorine is present
