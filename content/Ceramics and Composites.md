# Glass 
## Soda Lime Glass
Most glass is *soda-lime glass*. To make it mix: sand, sodium carbonate and limestone. Heat the mix in a furnace and melt. 
It has a low melting point.
## Borosilicate glass
Made by heating a mixture of sand and boron trioxide.

# Composites
Composites are made by combining two different materials:
1. The *reinforcement* which consist of fibres/fragments of one material
2. The matrix or binder material.

Composites have different properties to the materials in them.
