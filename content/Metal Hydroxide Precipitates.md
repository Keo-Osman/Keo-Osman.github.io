#Chemistry/Chemical-Analysis
You can test for metals by adding $NaOH$ or $NH_3$ and seeing what precipitate it forms.
Calcium, Zinc and Aluminium form a white precipitate. *Aluminium's and Zinc's will dissolve with excess $NaOH$. Zinc will dissolve with excess $NH_3$ as well*

Copper (ii) forms a blue precipitate.
Iron (ii) forms a green precipitate.
Iron (iii) forms a brown precipitate.
