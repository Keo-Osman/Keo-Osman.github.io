#Chemistry/Paper-2/C8__Chemical-Analysis
You can test for metals by adding $NaOH$ and seeing what precipitate it forms.
Calcium, Magnesium and Aluminium form a white precipitate. *Aluminium's will dissolve with excess $NaOH$.*
1. Copper (ii) forms a blue precipitate.
2. Iron (ii) forms a green precipitate.
3. Iron (iii) forms a brown precipitate.
