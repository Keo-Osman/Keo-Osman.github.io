# Regicide (Gunpowder Plot)  
The Gunpowder Plot was a plan to blow up King James I. There was conflict between Catholics and Protestants. It was revealed that some members of government were part of the conspiracy: James I was betrayed by members of Parliament.  
### Quotes
1. "Will all great Neptune’s ocean wash this blood clean from my hand?" - said by Macbeth when he realises the permanence of his crime after murdering Duncan.  
2. "There’s daggers in men’s smiles." - said by Donalbain when he recognises betrayal and danger hiding behind politeness.  
3. "O, yet I do repent me of my fury, that I did kill them." - said by Macbeth when he claims he killed Duncan’s guards out of loyalty but is actually covering his guilt.

# Witchcraft and King James I Daemonolgie  
King James I wrote about the dangers of witches and ordered a law whereby anyone seen fraternising with witches or anyone casting spells would be sentenced to death.  
### Quotes
1. "Double, double toil and trouble; fire burn and cauldron bubble." - said by the witches when they brew a spell to manipulate Macbeth.  
2. "By the pricking of my thumbs, something wicked this way comes." - said by the second witch when she senses Macbeth's approach, showing his descent into evil.  
3. "Come, you spirits that tend on mortal thoughts, unsex me here." - said by Lady Macbeth when she calls on dark forces to strip away her femininity.

# Great Chain of Being  
The belief that all of the universe was a hierarchy dictated by God, with God at the top followed by angels, right down to plants and animals at the bottom. The King was the highest representative of God on earth. Everyone was expected to stay in their place.  
### Quotes
1. "Most sacrilegious murder hath broke ope the Lord’s anointed temple." - said by Macduff when he describes Duncan’s body as a sacred temple destroyed by regicide.  
2. "Thou hast it now: King, Cawdor, Glamis, all, as the weird women promised, and I fear thou played’st most foully for’t." - said by Banquo when he suspects Macbeth has disrupted the natural order to gain power.  
3. "The heavens, as troubled with man's act, threaten his bloody stage." - said by ross when he describes how the natural world reflects Duncan's murder.

# Divine Right of Kings  
People believed that Kings and Queens were put on the throne by God. By betraying the King or Queen, you were going against God.  
### Quotes
1. "God’s benison go with you, and with those that would make good of bad and friends of foes!" - said by old man when he reflects on the unnatural events following regicide.  
2. "His silver skin laced with his golden blood." - said by Macbeth when he describes Duncan’s body, using heavenly imagery to show his sacred status.  
3. "It is a knell that summons thee to heaven or to hell." - said by Macbeth when he prepares to kill Duncan, highlighting the religious consequences.

# Role of Women  
Women were presented in literature and art to be passive and do as they were told by their fathers and husbands. They were seen as inferior to men. Women were expected to be wives and mothers.  
### Quotes
1. "When you durst do it, then you were a man." - said by Lady Macbeth when she manipulates Macbeth’s masculinity to spur him into killing Duncan.  
2. "Come to my woman’s breasts, and take my milk for gall." - said by Lady Macbeth when she wishes to rid herself of nurturing qualities.  
3. "Leave all the rest to me." - said by Lady Macbeth when she asserts control over the plot to murder Duncan, reversing expected gender roles.
