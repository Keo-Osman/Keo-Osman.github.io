#Physics/Electromagnetism 
*In summary it's the reverse of what happens in a [[Loudspeakers|Loudspeaker]]*

A coil of wire is wrapped around a permanent magnet and the coil is connected to the diaphragm. When sound waves push the diaphragm which causes the coil to move in and out of the magnetic field, this induces a potential difference and current via the [[Generator Effect]] which can be recorded
