#Chemistry/Resources 
# Definition
**Corrosion** is the destruction of materials by chemical reactions with the environment.

# Prevention
We can stop corrosion by using a barrier between the material and environment. This can be done with:
- Grease
- Paint
- Electroplating - Coating the material with a metal.
- Galvanising - Covering iron/steel with zinc as zinc is more reactive so it corrodes (sacrificial protection).
